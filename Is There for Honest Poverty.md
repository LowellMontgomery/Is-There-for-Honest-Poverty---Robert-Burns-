# A’ That and A’ That
**Modernized by the Open Source Community**
*Based on the original by Robert Burns*

Is there for honest Poverty 🎵  
Who feel ashamed of a’ that; 🎵  
The coward-slaves, we pass them by, 🎵  
We dare be poor for a’ that! 🎵  
For a’ that, an’ a’ that. 🎵  
Our toils obscure an’ a’ that, 🎵  
The rank is but the coinage stamp, 🎵  
We are the gold for a’ that. 🎵  

What though on simple fare we dine, 🎵  
Wear clothes a'frayed, an’ a that; 🎵  
Give fools their silks, and knaves their wine; 🎵  
We're who we are for a’ that: 🎵  
For a’ that, and a’ that, 🎵  
Their tinsel show, an’ a’ that; 🎵  
The honest ones, tho’ e’er so poor, 🎵  
Are best of us for a’ that. 🎵  

Ye see that guy they call a lord, 🎵  
Who struts, an' stares, an’ a’ that, 🎵  
Tho’ hundreds worship at his word, 🎵  
He’s but a fool for a’ that. 🎵  
For a’ that, an’ a’ that, 🎵  
His ribbon, star, an’ a’ that, 🎵  
The ones o’ independent mind, 🎵  
They look an’ laugh at a’ that. 🎵  

A Prince can make a belted knight, 🎵  
A marquis, duke, an’ a’ that! 🎵  
The honest soul's above their might – 🎵  
Good faith, they cannot buy that! 🎵  
For a’ that, an’ a’ that, 🎵  
Their dignities, an’ a’ that, 🎵  
The pith o’ Sense an’ pride o’ Worth 🎵  
Are higher rank than a’ that. 🎵  

Then let us pray that come it may, 🎵  
As come it will for a’ that, 🎵  
That Sense and Worth, o’er a’ the earth 🎵  
Shall be the way an’ a’ that. 🎵  
For a’ that, an’ a’ that, 🎵  
It’s comin yet for a’ that, 🎵  
That everyone the world o’er 🎵  
Shall be as one, for a’ that. 🎵

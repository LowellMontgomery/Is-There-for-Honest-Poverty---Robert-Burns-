# Contributing to "Is There for Honest Poverty"

Welcome! This project is a living effort to modernize Robert Burns' masterpiece for a 2026 audience while honoring its original spirit. We treat poetry like open-source software—it belongs to everyone, and it can always be improved.

## 🌟 Our Philosophy
We aim for **Universal Inclusivity**. Robert Burns wrote about the "Brotherhood of Man"; we write for the kinship of all living beings. 

## 🛠 How You Can Help
1.  **Translations:** Help us "port" this poem into other languages (French, Spanish, Gaelic, etc.) using inclusive, modern phrasing.
2.  **Dialect Refactoring:** Feel free to suggest "PRs" that either preserve more of the original Scots or provide "Plain English" versions for non-native speakers.
3.  **Meter & Rhythm:** If you find a line that is hard to sing to the traditional tune, suggest a rhythmic fix!

## 🚀 Submission Guidelines
* **Maintain the Meter:** The song is a 4/4 march. Ensure your lines don't "break the beat."
* **Preserve the Spirit:** Keep the focus on "Sense and Worth" being superior to titles and wealth.
* **Inclusive Language:** Avoid gendered or exclusionary terms unless they are vital to a specific historical context.

## 📜 Versioning
Please commit your changes with clear messages (e.g., "Updated Stanza 4 for better vocal flow" or "Added Modern German Translation").
